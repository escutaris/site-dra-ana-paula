Does this happen in your team?

When the pressure on a team reaches the leader first.

These are the situations HR professionals and leaders bring most often. Each session starts from the complaint or challenge the person points out.

Team overload

A workload that does not fit the team and reaches the leader before it becomes a declared problem.

Conflict and communication

Friction between people, conversations that do not happen and messages that land differently from how they were said.

Delegation and feedback

Distributing work without overloading, and saying what needs to be said in a way the person can use.

Change management

Leading a reorganization, a process change or a change of course without the team paying for it in wear and tear.

Supporting employees

Noticing who is suffering, knowing what is your role and what belongs to another area, and acting in time.

Sensitive situations

What to do when the subject is delicate and there is no ready-made protocol to follow.

If you recognize one of these situations, the first step is to understand the context, not to apply a standard solution.

The right fit

What it is, and what it is not.

What it is NOT

  • It is not a clinical diagnosis.
  • It is not a psychological assessment.
  • It is not a formal performance review.
  • It does not replace individual clinical care.
  • It does not replace legal advice, nor does it determine causal link or responsibility in specific cases.
  • It does not guarantee that sick leave or conflicts will stop happening.

What it IS

  • A self-assessment with the SMCIT, the Stress Management Competency Indicator Tool.
  • An individual report generated from your answers.
  • A 60-minute feedback session with an occupational physician.
  • Development of skills to prevent and manage stressors.
How it works

From the initial conversation to the feedback session.

01

Initial conversation, short

You tell me what is happening and together we assess whether Executive Guidance is the right format for your needs, or whether another path is a better fit.

02

Preliminary step, 30 minutes

You receive the SMCIT questionnaire and answer it. In the same session we identify the main complaint or challenge, and the individual report is generated from your answers.

03

Feedback session, 60 minutes

It takes place one to two weeks after you receive the report, in an individual session, in person in Salvador or on a virtual platform. The recording is private, for later reference.

What to expect

The goal is not to hand you a ready-made answer. It is to help you decide better.

  • More clarity about which stressors weigh on your team and on the way you lead.
  • Priorities organized from your individual report.
  • Tools to handle the next conversations with your team.
  • An occupational physician's view of what is your role and what calls for another area.

Every situation has its own characteristics. The outcome depends on the organization's context and on the information you bring to the session.

Before booking

Questions that come before the yes.

Who is Executive Guidance for?

For HR professionals and leaders who want to develop the skills to prevent and manage the stressors in their own team. The work is individual: each path is done with one person.

Is it in person or online?

Both. The feedback session can be in person in Salvador, at Clínica Kirinonomori Serviços e Saúde in Parque Bela Vista, or on a virtual platform.

How long does it take and how many sessions are there?

After the initial conversation, there are two sessions: the 30-minute preliminary step and the 60-minute feedback session. There are one to two weeks between them, and the full cycle usually takes about three weeks.

What do I need to prepare beforehand?

Nothing beyond a sense of what is happening. In the initial conversation you describe the context, and the SMCIT questionnaire is only sent after that.

Does it include a report or an action plan?

It includes an individual report, generated from your answers to the SMCIT, which is the basis for the feedback session. The feedback session is recorded, and you keep the recording for later reference.

What if the situation calls for a different kind of support?

You will be told clearly. When the need calls for individual clinical care or an assessment of the whole organization, this is pointed out in the initial conversation or in the feedback session.
