Executive Guidance · Individual assessment and mentoring

Pressure on a team calls for more than an improvised response.

When mental health, working conditions and management decisions intersect, acting without understanding the context increases uncertainty. Executive Guidance helps leaders and HR professionals recognize how their own way of managing affects the team's stress and define next steps with technical criteria.

The process combines the SMCIT, a self-assessment questionnaire, with individual mentoring led by Dr. Ana Paula Teixeira, occupational physician.

Does this happen in your team?

When Executive Guidance makes sense.

A leader needs to talk to a team member who is suffering and does not know how to handle the situation.

The team is dealing with overload or conflict, and the person in charge does not know where to start.

HR wants to prepare a leader who receives recurring complaints from the team.

The company is reorganizing work and wants its leaders to manage the change without making the team sick.

A leader wants to understand, with method, where they need to grow in order to protect the team.

Every situation has its own characteristics. The initial conversation helps understand the need and assess whether Executive Guidance is the right format, or whether a different kind of support is needed.

What it is

What Executive Guidance is.

It is an individual process, in two steps, to develop leaders' skills in preventing and managing stress in their team.

The SMCIT (Stress Management Competency Indicator Tool) was created by the Health and Safety Executive, the UK's workplace health and safety regulator, in partnership with the CIPD. It describes 66 leadership behaviors, grouped into 4 competencies associated with preventing stress in the team. The mentoring with the occupational physician starts from this result to discuss your reality and define the next steps.

The goal is not to offer an automatic answer or to replace the company's internal processes. It is to help leaders better understand their own role and handle the next steps with more clarity.

How it works

Two steps, with the report in between.

01

Preliminary step, 30 minutes: the SMCIT

You answer the SMCIT, a self-assessment in which you say how much you agree with 66 statements about the way you manage people. In the same session, we identify the main complaint or challenge. The result shows your own perception: it does not assess the team, it does not measure anyone's health and it is not a performance review.

You receive:

  • Guidance on how to answer.
  • An individual report with your percentage in each of the 4 competencies and 12 sub-competencies, and the band for each one: Effective, Reasonable or Needs development.
02

Feedback session, 60 minutes

One to two weeks after you receive the report, the individual session with Dr. Ana Paula Teixeira takes place, in person in Salvador (Clínica Kirinonomori, Parque Bela Vista) or online. The conversation reads your result together with you and connects it to the challenge you brought: what weighs on the team, where to act first and what is your role or another area's.

You receive:

  • A reading of your report, together with the occupational physician.
  • Development priorities for the coming months.
  • The recording of the session, made available to you for later reference.

The materials do not replace a medical report, expert opinion, organizational assessment or legal advice.

Before booking

Frequently asked questions.

What is the SMCIT?

It is the Stress Management Competency Indicator Tool (SMCIT), created by the UK's workplace health and safety regulator with the CIPD. It is a self-assessment of 66 leadership behaviors associated with preventing stress in the team. It is not a diagnosis and it does not predict illness.

Does the SMCIT replace a clinical or organizational assessment?

No. It does not replace individual clinical care, a psychological assessment, a performance review, an organizational psychosocial assessment or legal advice.

Who answers the questionnaire?

The person being served: the leader or the HR professional.

What happens after the questionnaire?

The individual report is generated from your answers. One to two weeks after you receive it, the 60-minute feedback session takes place, with a joint reading of the result.

Is it in person or online?

Both. The feedback session can be in person in Salvador or online.

Is the feedback session a medical report or an expert opinion?

No. It is a 60-minute session about your SMCIT report, recorded so you can refer back to it. It is not equivalent to a medical report, an expert opinion or an assessment of the company.
