Before requesting a proposal

Questions that come before the yes.

Does Dr. Ana Paula speak at events outside Salvador?

Yes. There is an in-person format for the Salvador metropolitan area and another for other capitals and inland cities, with travel, accommodation and per diem defined according to the city. For remote audiences, there is also the online format for up to 300 people.

What is included when you book a talk?

All formats include the recording sent to the client and the slides in PDF for internal distribution. For in-person talks in Salvador, the organizer also receives a signed copy of Quando o Trabalho Dói.

How long is the talk?

Online formats last about 60 minutes. In-person talks run up to 90 minutes, including time for audience questions.

Can the topic be adjusted to the event?

Yes. Each topic is adjusted to the audience, the goal and the organization's moment. There are also ready-made angles for SIPAT, leadership meetings and internal weeks.

How is the fee defined?

The proposal is built per event and considers format, audience size, city and materials delivered. Once you send the event context, the reply comes with a recommended topic, format and fee.

How quickly are requests answered?

Requests sent through the form are answered within 2 business days. On WhatsApp, the reply is usually faster. You can also book a 20-minute call directly in the calendar.
