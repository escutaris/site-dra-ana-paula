Who the data controller is

The controller of the personal data processed on this website is Ana Paula Cunha Teixeira, occupational physician (CRM-BA 12797 · RQE 7237), acting in her own name as a self-employed professional offering talks, consulting, training and editorial content on occupational health, NR-1 and Occupational Moral Injury.

This policy applies to all interactions made through the domain draanapaulateixeira.com.br and its subdomains, including forms, newsletter sign-ups and subsequent communications.

What data is collected

We collect only the data strictly necessary to reply to your message, confirm your subscription to content, or prepare a commercial proposal. No sensitive data (health data, racial origin, political opinion, religious affiliation, biometric data) is collected through this website.

Category Data When
Identification Full name, position, company Contact forms and proposal request forms

Technical IP address, browser type, pages visited, date and time Automatically, while browsing

Data submitted through the forms is stored in Google Forms / Google Sheets, linked to the controller's professional account. Technical browsing data is handled by the website's hosting provider and, where applicable, by the traffic analysis tool (Google Analytics, in anonymized form).

What we use your data for

  • Replying to contact requests: getting back to you through the channel given in the form or message.
  • Preparing commercial proposals: analyzing the context of the request (size, sector, deadline) and drafting a suitable proposal.
  • Sending editorial communications: if you voluntarily subscribed to the newsletter, sending the agreed content at the stated frequency.
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations: where applicable, in response to orders from competent authorities.
  • Improving the website experience: understanding, in aggregate and anonymized form, which pages are visited most.

The data is not used for targeted advertising, sale to third parties, or any purpose other than those described above.

On what legal basis we process data

Under Article 7 of the LGPD, personal data is processed on one of the following grounds:

  • Consent (Art. 7, I): when you voluntarily fill in a contact form or subscribe to the newsletter.
  • Pre-contractual procedures (Art. 7, V): when processing is necessary to prepare a commercial proposal you requested.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 7, IX): for aggregate technical analysis and website security, always respecting your expectations and rights.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation (Art. 7, II): when retention or sharing is required by law or court order.

Who we share it with

Your data is not sold, rented or transferred to third parties for commercial purposes. It may be shared only with:

  • Contracted processors that make the website and our service possible: Google (Forms, Sheets, Gmail), the hosting provider and the email marketing platform, all bound by data processing agreements compatible with the LGPD.
  • Public authorities, when required by law or court order.

Under no circumstances is your data transferred internationally to countries without an adequate level of protection, outside the conditions set out in the LGPD.

How long we keep it

Data is kept for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described:

  • Business contacts that did not result in a contract: up to 24 months after the last contact.
  • Contacts that resulted in a contract: for the legal tax and contractual retention period, typically 5 years after the relationship ends.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: while the subscription is active. Unsubscribing is immediate and available in every email sent.
  • Technical browsing data: up to 6 months.

We adopt reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration or improper disclosure: storage in corporate accounts with two-factor authentication, transmission over encrypted channels (HTTPS), and access restricted to the controller.

Your rights as a data subject

The LGPD guarantees you, as the data subject, the following rights, which may be exercised free of charge at any time:

  • Confirmation that we process your data.
  • Access to the data we hold about you.
  • Correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data.
  • Anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data, or data processed in breach of the LGPD.
  • Portability of data to another provider, in the applicable legal cases.
  • Deletion of data processed on the basis of your consent.
  • Information about the entities with which we share your data.
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time.
  • Objection to processing based on legitimate interest, in the event of non-compliance with the law.
How to exercise them

Send your request to escutaris@zohomail.com with the subject "LGPD: Data subject request". We reply within 15 calendar days, as required by law. We may ask for proof of identity before handling requests involving sensitive or identified data.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website uses cookies that are strictly necessary for browsing (for example, to remember display preferences) and aggregate analytics cookies (Google Analytics, when active), which do not identify you individually.

You can set your browser at any time to refuse cookies or to warn you before accepting them. Refusing may affect some website features, but does not prevent you from browsing the main pages.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect regulatory, technological or process changes. The version and effective date are shown at the beginning of this document.

Material changes will be clearly flagged on the website's home page and, where applicable, communicated directly to data subjects whose data is being actively processed.

Data protection officer (DPO)

Under Article 41 of the LGPD, the controller acts directly as the point of contact for personal data protection matters and has not appointed an external officer, given the volume and nature of the processing (a self-employed professional, without large-scale operations).
