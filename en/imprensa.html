Half page

Dr. Ana Paula Teixeira is an occupational physician with almost thirty years of clinical and corporate practice in mining, steel, laboratories, hospitals, media and retail companies. A researcher in Occupational Moral Injury in Brazil, in her master's degree at Escola Bahiana she leads the translation and validation of the OMIS-R scale for Brazilian Portuguese, one of the first studies on the subject in the country. She is the author of Quando o Trabalho Dói: do adoecimento silencioso à cultura de bem-estar (When Work Hurts: from silent illness to a culture of well-being, AssedioNet Publishing, 2025, 253 pages), nominated for the 7th Ecos da Literatura Award. She is a member of the International Commission on Occupational Health (ICOH), ABERGO and ANAMT/AMB, and a Board Member certified by Board Academy in 2024. She brings to companies, industry events and professional associations a clinical reading of the issues that now reach the management table: NR-1 (Brazil's occupational risk management standard) and psychosocial factors, workplace bullying, mental health-related illness, return to work and psychological safety. She has worked with organizations such as PetroRecôncavo, Alvopetro, ABRH-BA and ABRH Sergipe.

Fact-checking notes

Her registration is with the Regional Council of Medicine (CRM), not the Council of Psychology (CRP). She is a physician, not a psychologist.

Escutaris is the occupational mental health company of which she is a founding partner. In news stories, the name that signs is hers.

The research on Occupational Moral Injury is ongoing in her master's degree at Escola Bahiana. Results have not yet been published.
