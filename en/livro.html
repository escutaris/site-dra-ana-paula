About the book

To name clearly what makes people sick and what transforms.

Written for leaders, HR, occupational health professionals and readers who want a deeper understanding of the factors that sustain illness or drive transformation in organizations.

Who it is for

Four readers in mind.

  • 01 HR and people management who need technical language to bring the topic to the board.
  • 02 Leaders who see the impact of culture on their team's health and do not know where to start.
  • 03 Occupational physicians and occupational health teams looking for clinical and regulatory depth.
  • 04 Executives and the C-suite who have understood that mental health has become a matter of reputation, liability and governance.
The author

Almost three decades inside companies.

Dr. Ana Paula Teixeira is an occupational physician with almost thirty years of clinical and corporate practice in mining, steel, laboratories, hospitals, media and retail companies. A researcher in Occupational Moral Injury in Brazil, in her master's degree at Escola Bahiana she leads the translation and validation of the OMIS-R scale for Brazilian Portuguese, one of the first studies on the subject in the country.

Member of the International Commission on Occupational Health (ICOH), ABERGO and ANAMT/AMB. Certified Board Member 2024 by Board Academy. More than 130 talks delivered and more than 10,000 professionals trained, with 99% positive ratings.
